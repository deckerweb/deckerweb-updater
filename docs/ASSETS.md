Copyright © 2026 David Decker — DECKERWEB. The newly drawn updater artwork uses GPL-2.0-or-later, like the component. Editable SVG masters and PNG exports are in assets-github.

The design follows the approved deckerweb Library 1B family: blue-to-violet gradient, light plugin cards on the left, large white product lettering on the right and restrained corner circles. The updater adds its own update/download mark. Library raster pixels are not embedded or traced; the updater artwork is new vector construction in the same family. No stock image or third-party plugin icon is included.

GitHub banners: 1280 × 640, English and German, SVG/PNG. Icons: SVG plus 128 × 128 and 256 × 256 PNG, without language-specific text. Avenir Next/Arial are system-font references; no font files are bundled. PNGs are local exports. SVG lettering may use a fallback font on another system.

Three alternatives are retained under variants/A, B and C. Variant A is the selected design. Its icon card is centered; both arrow arcs share one circle with equal radii. The design is approved for publication. assets-github/design.json records palette, sizes and slogans. The SVG files remain the editable asset masters; regenerate PNGs after changes and inspect their layout. Source assets do not fetch remote resources.
